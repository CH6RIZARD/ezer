# 03. Complaint Management Policy

## 1. Why this matters

Partner banks are examined on complaints that arrive through their fintech partners. The CFPB uses complaints to target supervision and enforcement under its UDAAP authority (12 U.S.C. 5531, 5536). Complaints are also the earliest warning of a Reg E, Reg B or UDAAP problem. In a bank partnership the bank usually requires the program manager to log **every** complaint and report them monthly.

## 2. Definition

A **complaint** is any expression of dissatisfaction about an EZER product, service, fee, disclosure, employee or decision, from a customer or someone acting for them, through any channel. Tone, outcome, and whether the person uses the word "complaint" do not matter.

Examples: "you charged me twice", "my card was declined and I don't know why", "I deleted my account and you still emailed me", "why was I denied?", a 1-star app review that names a specific problem, a regulator or AG referral, or a post on social media.

A **simple inquiry** ("how do I freeze my card?") is not a complaint. If in doubt, log it as one.

## 4. Handling standards

| Step | Standard |
|---|---|
| Log | Same business day. Fields: ID, date received, channel, customer, product, category, description, and flags (Reg E error? Reg B/discrimination? UDAAP? privacy/data? regulator?), owner, actions, root cause, resolution, date closed, customer notified. |
| Acknowledge | Within `[DECISION: 1 business day]` |
| Escalate to the bank | Within **1 business day** for: regulator/AG/attorney complaints, alleged discrimination, data breaches, threatened litigation, media, or anything about the bank's product terms. `[DECISION: per bank agreement]` |
| Resolve | Within `[DECISION: 15 business days]`. If later, send the customer a written update. |
| Close | Written response explaining the outcome and the reasons, and where to go next (the bank, or the CFPB at consumerfinance.gov/complaint). |

**Specialist clocks override these standards:**
- A complaint that alleges an unauthorized or incorrect card or EFT transaction is a **Reg E error notice**, and its clock starts on receipt. Route it to 04 immediately.
- A dispute about information EZER furnishes to a credit bureau (if Pay-in-4 is ever reported) follows FCRA §623 (30 days). `[DECISION: will Pay-in-4 be furnished?]`
- A privacy rights request (access or deletion) goes to the 30-day process in the retention policy §5. Most of these are self-serve in app.

## 5. Regulatory complaints

- The bank receives complaints from the CFPB portal, OCC/FDIC/Fed, and state AGs. The CFPB expects a company response **within 15 days** and a final response **within 60 days**.
- EZER supplies facts and a draft response to the bank within `[DECISION: 5 business days]` of a request. The bank sends the final response.
- If a regulator contacts EZER directly: acknowledge receipt, do not substantively respond until the bank and counsel have been told (same day), and log it.

## 6. Root cause and reporting

- **Monthly to the bank:** volume by category and product, open items and age, escalations, root causes, and fixes.
- **Quarterly:** trend review by `[NAME: compliance owner]`. Look for repeat causes, especially disclosures that confuse people (see 08) and decisions customers do not understand (declines, Spending Power changes, suspensions after a missed Pay-in-4 installment).
- A complaint showing that a disclosure, screen or policy misdescribes what the product does is a UDAAP risk. Fix the screen or the code, not just the one customer's case.

## 7. Prohibited practices

- No retaliation, account closure or service downgrade because someone complained.
- Do not make "deleting the review" a condition of resolution.
- Do not reveal SAR or referral information (01 §6).
