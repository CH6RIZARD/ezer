EZER Subscription intelligence

You are paying for things you forgot about.

EZER reads your bank transactions, finds every recurring charge, and tells you which ones quietly went up in price — before the next one lands.

How it works

1

Connect your bank

Through Plaid, read-only. Your banking credentials go to Plaid, never to us — we never see them.

2

We find the pattern

Same merchant, same amount, same cadence. That's a subscription, whether or not you remember signing up.

3

You decide

Keep it, cancel it, or redirect what you save into a goal. EZER never cancels anything on its own.

Your data

Read-only, and yours to delete

  • We never see your banking login. Plaid handles the connection; we receive a token, never credentials.
  • Read-only access. EZER cannot move money out of your bank, and cannot cancel anything on your behalf.
  • Email scanning is optional. If you connect a mailbox, we look only for receipts and renewal notices — never anything else, and we never send mail as you.
  • Delete everything, any time. One tap removes your account, and we revoke each bank connection with Plaid before erasing our own records.
  • We do not sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to anyone. It is not part of the business model.

The full detail is in our Privacy Policy and Data Retention and Disposal Policy.
