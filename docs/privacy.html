EZER (“EZER,” “we,” “us”) is a personal finance app that finds recurring subscriptions in your accounts, shows you what they cost, and helps you decide what to keep. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have.

EZER is operated by EZER, 7308 Forest Bend Drive, Parker, Texas.

2. Bank data and Plaid

We use Plaid Inc. to connect to your financial institution. When you link an account, you enter your credentials directly with Plaid — EZER never sees or stores your online banking username or password. Plaid returns an access token that we store encrypted, and we use it to read:

  • Transactions — to detect recurring charges, price increases, and trials converting to paid
  • Balances — to check funds before recommending or making a savings transfer, so we never cause an overdraft
  • Account details and ownership — to confirm a linked account belongs to you before any money movement

Plaid’s handling of your data is governed by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. You can disconnect a linked account at any time in EZER’s settings; we then instruct Plaid to revoke the connection and stop retrieving data.

3. Email access

Connecting a mailbox is optional. EZER works without it — it simply catches fewer subscriptions, because some never appear as a clean bank transaction.

If you connect one, we request read-only access and scan only for subscription and billing signals: merchant, amount, renewal date, trial end date, and price changes. We do not read unrelated correspondence, and we never send email on your behalf. You can disconnect at any time in settings, or directly from your Google account permissions or Microsoft account.

Google API Services Limited Use disclosure. EZER’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Specifically: we use Gmail data only to provide user-facing subscription-detection features, we do not transfer it except as necessary to provide those features or as required by law, we do not use it for advertising, and no human reads it except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, or where required by law.

4. How we use your information

  • Detect and track your recurring subscriptions, trials, and price increases
  • Show what you spend and alert you before a renewal or trial conversion
  • Calculate savings recommendations and, where you enable it, move money toward your goals
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • Detect and prevent fraud and abuse
  • Provide support and fix problems
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use your financial or email data to target ads.

6. International transfers

Our servers and service providers are located in the United States. If you access EZER from outside the US, your information will be transferred to and processed there, under appropriate safeguards where required by law.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. Deleting your account removes your personal information — account details, bank connections, transactions, and email-derived subscription data — from our live database immediately. Encrypted backups still holding it are purged on a 30-day rotation, after which no copy remains.

Two things outlive that. Records of money movement (savings transfers and ledger entries) are kept for seven years to meet financial recordkeeping obligations, separated from your identifying details. Your consent record — when you accepted this policy and which version — is kept for three years as evidence that we had permission to process your data at all. Application logs rotate out after 30 days.

Our full Data Retention and Disposal Policy sets out every category and its period.

8. Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Financial access tokens are encrypted with a dedicated key, separate from the application database
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes — never in plain text, and never recoverable by us
  • Access to production systems is restricted and logged

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can do all of this yourself from inside the app, without asking us:

  • Download your data — a full export in a portable format. Credentials are excluded; we cannot read them either.
  • Delete your account — everything goes, and we revoke each bank connection with Plaid before removing our own records, so nothing is left connected to your bank.
  • Disconnect a bank or mailbox — stops collection from that source immediately.
  • Correct your details — in settings.

Depending on your location — including under the GDPR (EEA/UK) and the CCPA/CPRA (California) — you may also have the right to request a copy of your data in a portable format, request deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, and not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights. To make a request, contact us at the address below. We will respond within the timeframe the applicable law requires.

10. Children

EZER is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn we have, we will delete it and close the account.
